Who to contact

CK Upholstery, Unit 2, Esgors Farm, Epping, Essex, CM16 6LY. Email sales@ckupholstery.co.uk or call 07944 715635 about your personal information.

Enquiries

When you request a quote, we use the name, email, telephone number, location and message you provide to respond and discuss the work. This processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering a contract, or for our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

Our forms use Formspree to process and deliver enquiries. Vercel hosts the website. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and process data outside the UK using their applicable safeguards. We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting work, accounting obligations or disputes.

Cookies and measurement

When Google measurement is configured, you can choose whether to allow optional analytics and advertising measurement. Google tags load only after you accept. They may use cookies and ad click identifiers to measure visits and actions. Rejecting optional measurement does not prevent you from contacting us. Your choice is saved in your browser; use the cookie settings button to change it. We do not send form contents, email addresses or telephone numbers to analytics events.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, restrict processing or object to processing where applicable. Contact us using the details above. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
